# Medjugorje Marian iconography — research before generating a logo

### Primary source: parish's detailed 1992 questionnaire

https://archive.medjugorje.hr/en/medjugorje-phenomenon/detailed-description/

The parish reproduces Fr Janko Bubalo's questionnaire answered by five of the visionaries and accepted by the sixth. It describes a youthful, serene figure in a **freely flowing grey or slightly bluish-grey dress**, with a **pure white veil**, no belt or jewellery, **12 gold stars** around her head, and a small **whitish cloud** beneath her feet. Hands are normally relaxed, extended, with palms upward; blue eyes and a gently peaceful expression. These are reported descriptions, not a Church-certified supernatural portrait.

### Distinguish popular statue from reported description

The well-known **white-stone statue by St James's Church** shows Mary in long flowing robes with a gentle lowered gaze and one hand near her heart, the other open. Pilgrims may place a rose or rosary in its hands; these objects are not mandatory identity attributes from the reported description. Reference photographs occur in https://www.scross.co.za/2022/11/medjugorje-place-of-prayer-and-grace/ and https://www.americamagazine.org/faith/2017/05/18/whats-going-medjugorje-interview-priest-assigned-investigate-it ; use as references **only**, not as unlicensed image assets.

### Visual recommendation

For a **40px app header + 192/512 PWA icon**, create an original symbolic Marian monogram:
- pure-white veil contour, with a muted blue-grey dress/field
- elegant downcast profile or small, welcoming, upward-facing hands
- one discreet gold star/arc in header; a full set of twelve stars only at sizes where legible
- no gratuitous crown, sceptre, globe, dramatic rays, ornaments, angel wings or baroque patterns
- optional extremely understated cloud/peace motif
- soft Marian palette: ivory, pale blue-grey, muted slate, a *tiny* warm gold accent
- must read as a Marian symbol at 24–40px without tiny face detail; no text inside the square icon
- work on both dark and light backdrops, avoid dependence on a photograph
- credit as original illustration (if AI-generated, disclose this when relevant)

The Marian artwork is illustrative, **not** a claim about the historical accuracy of the visionary experiences. Do not use the Vatican's `nihil obstat` as confirmation of supernatural authenticity: https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_ddf_doc_20240919_nota-esperienza-medjugorje_en.html .
